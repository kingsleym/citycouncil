My Commitment to San Bruno

I am Kingsley and I am asking you for the opportunity to serve San Bruno as your City Council Member, representing District 1. San Bruno has given my family strong schools, safe neighborhoods, and a warm sense of community. I want to make sure we preserve what makes San Bruno special while addressing the challenges we face together.

As your Council Member, I will be passionate about attracting small businesses to San Bruno and creating housing that is affordable. I will advocate for continued partnership with our public schools and for the imperative to invest in the infrastructure that keeps our neighborhoods secure for future generations to come.

In my volunteer role with the San Bruno Education Foundation and in my elected capacity as President of the Board of Education for the San Bruno Park School District, we've seen our schools and students thrive. I am ready to bring that positive energy, vision and collaborative leadership to the San Bruno City Council. I won't accept politics as usual. You deserve a Council Member who shows up, listens, and who stays true to his values and commitment to get things done – for you and your families.

I humbly ask for your support in this November's election. Please do not hesitate to email with questions, suggestions, or concerns at votekingsleyma@gmail.com. Thank you.

About Me

I was born and raised in the Bay Area. I graduated from the University of California San Diego where I received my Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science. I have worked for the past twenty years in technology companies in the areas of software development, product management, and project management.

In 2024 I was elected by the voters in San Bruno to serve on the San Bruno Park School District's Board of Trustees and in 2025, elected by the Board as President. Additionally, I volunteer with the San Bruno Education Foundation.

In my professional career and in my service on the school board, I have been a strong advocate for under-represented minorities. My success in the corporate sector and as an elected official is the result of being a hands-on leader with an emphasis on cooperative teamwork towards a shared vision.

My wife has a master's degree in teaching, Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) specializing in young children with autism. We have two children enrolled in the San Bruno Park School District: eight-year-old (third grade, Portola) and a 12-year-old (7th grade, Parkside).
