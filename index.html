My Commitment to San Bruno

I'm Kingsley and I'm grateful for the opportunity to serve San Bruno because I believe our city deserves leadership that listens, acts, and delivers results. San Bruno has given my family good schools, safe neighborhoods, and a strong sense of community. I want to make sure we preserve what makes San Bruno special while addressing the challenges we face together.

As your council member, I'm committed to bringing in housing that is affordable, attracting small businesses and companies, continuing to partner closely with our public schools, and investing in the infrastructure that keeps our neighborhoods secure for future generations.

As a governing board member for San Bruno Park School District, I've seen our schools and students thrive, and I'm ready to bring that positive energy and a fresh perspective as your council member. I won't accept politics as usual. You deserve a council member who shows up, listens, and gets things done. Together, we can build a stronger, more affordable, more prosperous community for everyone.

I humbly ask for your support this November election. Please email me at votekingsleyma@gmail.com. Thank you.
